Dynamic Risk Scoring Equation

NetSentry AI synthesizes multiple telemetry indicators into a normalized 0 to 100 Risk Score. Each factor is dynamically weighted based on SOC policy configurations:

Risk = (wiso × IsoScore) + (wrf × RFClass) + (wbeh × BaselineDev) + (wvol × VolumeAnom) + (wrep × ThreatRep)
Component Indicator Active Weight Evaluation Logic
Isolation Forest (IsoScore) 30% Unsupervised high-dimensional statistical anomaly distance
